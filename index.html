The platform

Three apps. One connected campus.

Each person gets an app built for their role, with no clutter. Behind them sits a single secure system.

FaculZo Student

Everything a student needs, in the pocket.

  • Notes, assignments and notices by branch and year
  • Leave requests sent to the right teacher
  • QR gate pass once approved
  • Timetable, exams and attendance
For students

FaculZo Staff

Teach, post and approve without paperwork.

  • Publish notes, assignments and notices
  • Approve or reject leave in one tap
  • Share PDFs with the right class
  • Manage timetable and attendance
For teachers

FaculZo Security

Control at the gate and across accounts.

  • Scan and verify gate passes, one use only
  • Create and manage student and staff accounts
  • Block, reset or remove users
  • Gate log and alerts
For security and admin
How it works

From request to gate, in four steps.

Student asks

Sends a leave request to a chosen teacher.

Teacher decides

Approves or rejects. The answer appears in the student's app.

Pass is issued

An approved request becomes a single-use QR pass.

Gate verifies

Security scans it. It works once, today only.

Security and privacy

Built private from the first line.

Your campus data belongs to your campus. FaculZo is made for closed communities, not the open internet.
