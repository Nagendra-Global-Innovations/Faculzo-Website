FaculZo Privacy Policy

Applies to FaculZo Student, FaculZo Staff and FaculZo Security. Last updated: 22 November 2026.

FaculZo is made by Nagendra Global Innovations (NGI) for colleges. A college gives its students, teachers and gate security staff their logins. FaculZo handles the data only to run the college's app, on the college's behalf.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name, title, username, role, branch and yearTo sign you in and show you the right content
PasswordTo sign you in. It is stored in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody can read it afterwards, including us and your college. When an account is created or its password is reset, Security sees the temporary password once and gives it to the person, who should change it after the first sign-in.
Attendance, timetable, exam datesTo show attendance and the exam countdown
Leave requests (date, reason, teacher, decision) and gate pass scansTo approve leave, issue the gate pass and tell the teacher when the student leaves
Posts, notices and PDF files uploaded by teachersTo share notes and assignments with the right students
Notification address of your phone (only if you turn notifications on)To send you alerts such as "Leave approved"
Camera (Security app only)To scan gate pass QR codes. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads and do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use your data to build profiles for other companies.

Who can see your data

Only people at your own college who need it: a student sees their own records; a teacher sees their own posts, leave requests and the attendance they marked; Security sees logins and gate records. Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, and this website is hosted by Cloudflare. If you turn notifications on, your phone's browser delivers them through its own notification service. Apart from these service providers, we share data with others only if the law requires it.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules make sure a person can only read what their role allows. Blocked accounts are shut out immediately.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while your college uses FaculZo. To correct or delete your data, ask your college's Security desk or email us at the address below. We delete a person's login and records when the college asks us to, and all of a college's data when its contract ends.

Children

FaculZo is for college students, teachers and staff. Accounts are created only by the college. We do not let anyone sign up on their own and we do not knowingly collect data from children outside a college that has set them up.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date appears at the top.
